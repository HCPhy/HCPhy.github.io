Conway's Game of Life

Draw or erase cells, then press Start. Use the arrow keys to move the focused cell and Space or Enter to toggle it.

Use arrow keys to move across the grid. Press Space or Enter to switch the focused cell between alive and dead.

Random Coupling Model

Toggle lattice sites to replace a five-body constraint with a one-body coupling, then inspect the resulting GF(2) solution.

Processing time scales with grid size cubed (N3).

Use arrow keys to move across the lattice. Press Space or Enter to toggle the focused coupling.
